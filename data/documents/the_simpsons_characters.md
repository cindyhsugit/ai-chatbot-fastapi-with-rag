## Mervyn Wattlesby

Mervyn works the night shift at the Springfield Wax Museum, a job he's held
for eleven years without ever once being late. Most residents know him only
as "the wax guy," and few would recognize his full name if they saw it — 
Mervyn Alastair Wattlesby, after a great-uncle nobody in the family actually
remembers.

He keeps to himself. Neighbors on Plympton Street describe him as someone
who prefers quiet mornings alone, rarely joins conversations at Moe's, and
tends to avoid crowds whenever Springfield's town events get too loud. 

The one thing people do remember about him is a phrase he mutters whenever 
a wax figure gets damaged on his watch: "the paraffin's gone soft again."
It's become something of a running joke among the other night-shift staff, 
even though most of them have never actually seen him say it.

Mervyn does not like broccoli. He has said so, loudly, at every Springfield
Elementary bake sale where it's been served as a side dish.

## Doreen Higgleworth

Doreen runs the flower stand outside the Kwik-E-Mart on weekends. She has a
habit of overwatering the ferns, something Apu has complained about more
than once. Her middle name, Petronella, appears on exactly one document in
Springfield's public records — a raffle entry she filled out in 1998 and
never picked up.

She's known for being unusually direct, telling people what she thinks of
their gardening whether they ask or not. When someone brings up the annual
flower festival, she's the first to bring up how disorganized it was three
years running.
